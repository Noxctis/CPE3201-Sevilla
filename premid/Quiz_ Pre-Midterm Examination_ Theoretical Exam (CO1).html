Quiz Instructions

Target Outcome: (CO1) Apply the concepts and fundamentals to answer or formulate solutions to function-specific problems in embedded systems.

Perfect Score: 100 pts           Target score: 85% & above

Instructions:

Written Part I. Select the best answer. (1 pt per item, 20 pts)
Written Part II. Submit or encode the required document or information. (10 pts per item, 50 pts) [see rubric below]

Use draw.io Links to an external site. or a similar application to draw any illustration(s) required.

Note: Annotate your name on the document(s) to be submitted.

Criteria Outstanding Competent Marginal Not Acceptable
Content The main ideas presented to answer the questions given are clear and supported with detailed and accurate information. The main ideas to answer the questions given are clear, however, are not supported by detailed information. The main ideas presented are somewhat clear. Information presented does not entirely support the idea. The main ideas and information presented are not clear.
Organization

The ideas are well organized; presented in a logical order. Stated clearly the ideas in a strong manner

Ideas are presented in organized, logical order.

Ideas are presented with lesser organization.

No organization in the ideas were observed.

During the exam:

  • Use of personal phones, tablets, computers and other similar items are prohibited.
  • Communication with other people, in any form, online or offline, is not allowed while the examination is ongoing. Concerns must be addressed only to the instructor.
  • "Googling", searching for answers, use of AI tools and other similar acts are not allowed. Use the internet only to access Canvas and draw.io.
  • Access to software and files in the computer is limited to what the instructor specifies.

Assessment:

  • Answers will be verified, validated and graded according to the rubrics above. Cheating, in any form, will be strictly dealt with. Students found guilty will be subject to disciplinary actions in accordance to the University Student Manual.
